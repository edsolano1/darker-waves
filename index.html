Merch and food

Merch

12:15 to 1:15 PM

Go first thing, while lines are short and sizes are in stock. The closest tent is on the main path from the entrance, just past the Arcade. A second one sits on the beach side, west of the VIP lounge.

Clear bags only, so a locker is worth it if you buy more than a shirt.

Food

2:10 to 2:40 PM

The festival hasn't posted its food vendors on the site or the map yet. The map shows food at five spots. The one between Waves and Black Tide is closest to your sets.

  • Go for a plate with beans, tofu or a veggie patty for the protein.
  • Skip anything with olives.
  • The festival is cashless, so bring a card or phone.
  • No outside food. An empty plastic bottle is fine for the water stations.

Set times and the map come from the festival's own schedule and help center, both updated Nov 11. The festival says times can change, so give the schedule one last look Saturday morning.
